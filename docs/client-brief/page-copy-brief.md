# Copy for Harry

Pages:

**Home**

1\. Hero (first screen, no scrolling)

* One full-width photo of a finished garden lit at dusk. This image carries the whole brand.  
* A headline of one short line about what you do (something like "Garden lighting, designed and installed to the highest standard").  
* A sub-line covering who it's for and where you work.  
* Two buttons: "Book a free consultation" (phone or email) and the phone number itself.  
* *Assets:* your best hero image (ideally landscape and portrait crops for mobile), logo files, and the headline and sub-line copy.

2\. Short intro (2-3 sentences)  
 Say what makes you different: premium materials, qualified electricians, a tailored design for each garden. Keep it about the client's outcome, not your company history.

* *Assets:* the copy.

3\. What we do (3-4 short blocks)  
 For example: lighting design, installation, outdoor power and sockets, and controls or smart systems. Each block gets an icon or small photo and one line. Link them all to the Garden Lighting page.

* *Assets:* a photo or icon per block, and the copy.

4\. How it works (3-4 steps)  
 Free consultation, design and quote, installation, aftercare. This is where the free consultation does its work, because it makes the first step feel low-risk.

* *Assets:* the step copy, and any icons.

5\. Featured projects (2-3)  
 One large image each, with a short caption: the type of garden, and what you did. Link to the full Projects page.

* *Assets:* your best 2-3 projects, with a few lines of write-up for each. If you're shooting these yourself, get them at dusk or just after sunset, with a few wide shots and a few detail shots.

6\. Trust strip  
 Qualifications, accreditations, insurance, guarantees, and a client quote or two if you have them. If you're new, lean on the accreditations and guarantees and add testimonials as they come in.

* *Assets:* scheme logos, insurance details, quote text with the client's permission.

7\. Closing call to action  
 A repeat of the hero call to action with the phone number, email, and opening hours.

Header and footer: the header carries the logo, five nav links, and the phone number and email button. The footer repeats contact details, service area, and social links.
